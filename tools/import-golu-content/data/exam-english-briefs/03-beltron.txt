// BELTRON — DEO (fictional District Data Services Centre)

#BELTRON-001 | entering citizen details into a service portal
- The operator enters the applicant's name exactly as written on the identity document attached to the form.
- Mandatory fields on the portal are marked, and the form cannot be saved until each of them is filled.
- The mobile number is entered twice by the system's design, which helps catch a single mistyped digit.
- After saving, the portal shows an application number that the operator writes on the paper form.

#BELTRON-002 | checking names and dates in application records
- Names are checked letter by letter, because a single wrong letter can make a record impossible to find.
- Dates of birth are compared with the certificate and entered in the day, month and year order used by the portal.
- A record in which the date of birth appears later than the date of application is set aside for review.
- Corrections are made only through the edit option that keeps a log of the old and new values.

#BELTRON-003 | a district-wise beneficiary summary
- The summary lists each district with the number of beneficiaries approved, pending and rejected under a scheme.
- District totals are added to give a state total, which must match the figure in the portal report.
- Districts are listed in alphabetical order so that readers can find a district quickly.
- Any district whose figures change sharply from the previous month is marked for a check.

#BELTRON-004 | a certificate application receipt register
- The receipt register records every application for caste, income or residence certificates on the day it is received.
- Each line shows the token number, the applicant's name, the type of certificate and the date of receipt.
- The expected date of delivery is written beside each entry according to the service time limit.
- Applications returned for missing documents are marked with the reason and the date of return.

#BELTRON-005 | a public service centre token and queue log
- Each visitor receives a numbered token, and the token number is called out when a counter becomes free.
- The log records the time at which each token was issued and the time at which service began.
- Waiting times are worked out from the log at the end of the day and averaged for each counter.
- Counters with long waiting times are given extra staff during the busiest hours.

#BELTRON-006 | digitising paper forms into structured records
- Paper forms are sorted by type before entry begins, so that the operator uses one screen layout at a time.
- Every field on the paper form has a matching field on the screen, and nothing is entered in a free comment box by mistake.
- Illegible handwriting is not guessed; the form is marked for verification with the applicant.
- Each completed form is stamped as entered, with the date and the operator's code.

#BELTRON-007 | resolving duplicate entries in a database
- A duplicate is suspected when two records share the same name, date of birth and address.
- Before merging, the operator compares the application numbers and dates to find which record came first.
- The older record is usually kept, and the newer one is marked as a duplicate with a reference to the original.
- No record is deleted outright; the system keeps it with a duplicate flag for audit purposes.

#BELTRON-008 | a data entry quality checklist
- The checklist asks the operator to confirm that names, numbers and dates match the source document.
- It also asks whether every mandatory field is filled and whether the record has been saved successfully.
- A supervisor checks a sample of records from each operator every day using the same checklist.
- Errors found in the sample are discussed with the operator, so that the same mistake is not repeated.

#BELTRON-009 | a monthly summary of applications received
- The monthly summary shows the number of applications received for each service, week by week.
- The four or five weekly figures are added to give the total for the month.
- The summary compares the month's total with the previous month and notes any large change.
- It is prepared from the portal report and checked against the counter registers.

#BELTRON-010 | tracking pending and completed service requests
- Each service request is shown as received, under process, completed or rejected.
- Requests that remain under process beyond the time limit are listed separately for follow-up.
- The completion date is entered as soon as the certificate or reply is issued.
- A weekly report shows the number of requests in each status for every counter.

#BELTRON-011 | agricultural scheme beneficiary data
- Beneficiary records for a farm support scheme include the farmer's name, village, land area and bank account.
- Land area is entered in the unit used on the land record, such as hectares or acres, without conversion.
- Bank account numbers are entered with great care, because a wrong digit sends money to the wrong account.
- Records are verified by the block office before payments are released.

#BELTRON-012 | school enrolment and attendance data
- Enrolment data lists each class with the number of boys and girls admitted at the start of the session.
- Monthly attendance is entered as the number of days each pupil attended out of the working days.
- Totals for each class are added and compared with the school's own register.
- Any pupil absent for a long period is listed so that the school can contact the family.

#BELTRON-013 | health centre stock and visit records
- The stock record lists medicines and supplies with the quantity received, issued and remaining.
- The visit record shows the number of patients seen each day, divided into new and repeat visits.
- Both records are entered at the end of each day so that they stay up to date.
- Shortages shown in the stock record are reported to the district store in a weekly statement.

#BELTRON-014 | pension request records and verification status
- Each pension request record holds the applicant's details, the scheme and the documents submitted.
- Verification status is updated when the field officer confirms the applicant's age and residence.
- Requests waiting for a document are marked pending, with the name of the missing document.
- Approved requests move to the payment list, and the date of approval is entered in the record.

#BELTRON-015 | updating contact details after document review
- A change of address or mobile number is made only after the supporting document has been seen.
- The old details are not erased; the system keeps them in the history of the record.
- The date of the change and the operator's code are recorded with every update.
- The citizen receives a message confirming that the contact details have been changed.

#BELTRON-016 | a daily workload report for a DEO
- The daily workload report shows how many records the operator entered, verified and corrected.
- Time spent on other tasks, such as scanning or answering queries, is noted separately.
- The report is generated from the portal and signed by the supervisor at the end of the shift.
- Monthly totals from the daily reports are used to plan staffing for the following month.

#BELTRON-017 | data transfer between office registers
- When a manual register is replaced by a computer system, its entries are transferred in their original order.
- Each transferred entry carries the register volume and page number from which it was copied.
- A sample of entries is checked against the old register after every hundred lines.
- The old register is kept safely after transfer, since it remains the original record.

#BELTRON-018 | verifying numeric fields and reference IDs
- Numeric fields such as amounts, quantities and dates are checked for the correct number of digits.
- Reference IDs follow a fixed pattern of letters and numbers, and any ID that breaks the pattern is questioned.
- Leading zeros in an ID are kept, because removing them changes the identifier.
- The operator reads long numbers in groups of three or four to reduce transposition errors.

#BELTRON-019 | an online form submission error log
- The error log records each failed submission with the time, the form type and the message shown on screen.
- Common errors include a missing mandatory field, a wrong date format and an expired session.
- The operator corrects the cause and resubmits the form, noting the second attempt in the log.
- Errors that keep recurring are reported to the technical team with screenshots and the log entries.

#BELTRON-020 | a Bihar government service information notice
- The notice tells citizens which services are available at the centre and the documents each one needs.
- It gives the opening hours of the centre and the days on which it remains closed.
- Fees, where they apply, are listed beside each service in rupees.
- The notice ends with the helpline number and the address for complaints about service.

#BELTRON-021 | a district-level table of service delivery
- The table lists each block with the applications received, delivered within time and delivered late.
- A column shows the percentage of applications delivered within the time limit.
- Blocks are sorted by that percentage, so that the slowest blocks appear at the bottom.
- The table is checked by adding each column and comparing the totals with the district report.

#BELTRON-022 | scanning, naming and archiving office documents
- Documents are scanned at a resolution that keeps small print readable when the image is enlarged.
- Each scanned file is named with the department code, the file number and the date of the document.
- Scanned files are stored in folders arranged by year and month.
- A copy of every folder is backed up on a separate drive at the end of each week.

#BELTRON-023 | data confidentiality and responsible record access
- Operators access only the records needed for their assigned work.
- Passwords are never shared, and the computer is locked whenever the operator leaves the desk.
- Printed lists containing personal details are kept out of public view and shredded when no longer needed.
- Any accidental disclosure is reported to the supervisor at once.

#BELTRON-024 | a mixed passage of names, dates and numbers
- This practice passage mixes personal names, dates, amounts and reference numbers in ordinary sentences.
- The typist must keep capital letters in names and the exact spacing in dates and amounts.
- Numbers are not rounded or shortened; each one is typed exactly as it appears.
- Careful typists slow down slightly at each number and speed up again on plain words.

#BELTRON-025 | a timed data-entry practice passage
- The timed passage tests how steadily an operator can type without losing accuracy.
- Short words and long reference numbers alternate, as they do in real application forms.
- The operator should not stop to look back; it is better to keep moving and correct only when the rules allow.
- At the end, the speed and the number of errors are both reviewed before the next attempt.
