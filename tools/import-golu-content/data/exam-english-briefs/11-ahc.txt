// AHC — Allahabad High Court Group-C practice (fictional Devgarh District Court Registry)

#AHC-001 | receipt of a new file in a district court registry
- A new file reaching the registry is checked for the number of papers listed on its cover.
- The receipt is entered in the inward register with the date and the sending office.
- Papers missing from the file are recorded on a short note attached to the cover.
- The file is then passed to the section that will register it.

#AHC-002 | preparing a court cause list
- The cause list is compiled from the dates fixed in each case by the presiding officer.
- Fresh matters, part-heard matters and matters for orders are listed under separate headings.
- The list is proofread against the case diary before it is displayed.
- Copies are supplied to the bar association and the court reader.

#AHC-003 | a notice of hearing in formal English
- A notice of hearing informs a party of the date, the time and the court in which the matter will be heard.
- It names the case and the parties in full.
- The notice warns that the matter may be decided in the party's absence if no one appears.
- It is signed by the officer authorised to issue notices.

#AHC-004 | a court record index and page number verification
- The record index lists every document in the file with its page numbers.
- The clerk turns every page to confirm that the numbering is continuous.
- A missing page number is investigated before the file goes to the court.
- The verified index is signed and dated.

#AHC-005 | an application for a certified copy
- The application names the applicant, the case and the documents of which copies are wanted.
- It states whether the copies are needed urgently, with the urgency fee if applicable.
- The copying section gives the application a number and a likely date of delivery.
- The applicant collects the copies on that date against a signature.

#AHC-006 | dispatching summons to the right address
- The address on each summons is copied exactly from the plaint or the application for summons.
- Incomplete addresses are referred to the party for correction before dispatch.
- The dispatch register records the mode of service chosen.
- The process server's report is attached to the file on return.

#AHC-007 | updating case status and the next hearing date
- The case status is updated in the register as soon as the court passes an order.
- The next hearing date is copied from the order sheet and checked twice.
- The updated status is entered in the online system for the public.
- Parties who call the enquiry counter are given the date from the updated record.

#AHC-008 | a registry objection and compliance note
- A registry objection note lists defects such as missing signatures or deficient court fee.
- Each defect is numbered, and the time allowed for compliance is stated.
- When the party cures a defect, the date is written against that defect.
- The note is placed in the file and forms part of the record.

#AHC-009 | a record room issue register
- Every file issued from the record room is entered with the case number and the name of the person receiving it.
- The purpose of the issue, such as inspection or copying, is noted.
- Files are returned to the same shelf position from which they were taken.
- The register is checked weekly for files not yet returned.

#AHC-010 | a daily court workload summary
- The workload summary gives the number of matters listed, heard and adjourned in each court.
- Adjournments are classified by reason, such as absence of a party or of a witness.
- The summary is prepared from the court diaries at the end of the day.
- Monthly totals are worked out from the daily summaries.

#AHC-011 | a statement of pending applications
- The statement lists applications pending before each court with their filing dates.
- It shows how long each application has been pending, in months.
- Applications pending for a long time are highlighted for the attention of the court.
- The statement is prepared every month.

#AHC-012 | a court fee receipt and filing checklist
- The filing checklist confirms that the court fee has been paid and receipted.
- It also checks the vakalatnama, the index and the copies for the opposite party.
- The clerk ticks each item on the checklist before accepting the filing.
- The completed checklist is attached to the filing.

#AHC-013 | digital court records and e-filing
- E-filing allows documents to be submitted to the court without visiting the counter.
- Digital records can be searched by case number, party name or year.
- Scanned records must be clear enough to read every word and figure.
- Digital records are backed up regularly to prevent loss.

#AHC-014 | maintaining a list of court holidays
- The holiday list is prepared at the start of the year from the official calendar.
- Each holiday is listed with its date and the day of the week.
- Local holidays declared during the year are added to the list with the date of the order.
- The list is displayed in the registry and on the court website.

#AHC-015 | a notice for a public helpdesk
- The helpdesk notice tells visitors what help is available, such as case status and directions.
- It gives the helpdesk timings and the location within the court premises.
- The notice lists the documents a visitor should bring for an enquiry.
- It is written in simple language and displayed near the entrance.

#AHC-016 | reducing delay in case processing
- Delay often arises when summons are not served on time or files are not ready for hearing.
- Regular review of pending service reports reduces adjournments.
- Files are prepared a day before the hearing so that the court can take them up at once.
- Monthly reports help identify the stages at which delay is most common.

#AHC-017 | formal correspondence between court sections
- Correspondence between sections is carried on through short office notes.
- Each note states the case, the point to be dealt with and the action requested.
- Replies refer to the note by its date and number.
- Notes and replies are kept in the file to show the history of the matter.

#AHC-018 | names and addresses in judicial records
- Names in judicial records are written exactly as they appear in the pleadings.
- Addresses include the village or locality, the post office, the police station and the district.
- Changes of address are recorded only on an application by the party.
- Accurate addresses are essential for the service of notices.

#AHC-019 | preserving documents during scanning
- Documents are handled gently during scanning to avoid tearing old paper.
- Staples and pins are removed carefully and the papers re-fastened afterwards.
- Pages are scanned in their original order, and none are left out.
- The original file is returned to the record room in the same condition.

#AHC-020 | the confidentiality of parties' personal information
- Personal details of parties, such as phone numbers and addresses, are used only for court work.
- Such details are not read aloud at the counter.
- Records containing sensitive details are kept away from public view.
- Requests for personal information from outsiders are refused unless the court directs otherwise.

#AHC-021 | court staff attendance and duty roster
- The attendance register records the arrival of every staff member each morning.
- The duty roster assigns staff to courts, counters and record rooms.
- Absences are covered by staff from a reserve list.
- The roster is reviewed weekly and approved by the officer in charge.

#AHC-022 | a typed brief of an administrative order
- The brief summarises an administrative order in a few short paragraphs.
- It states what the order requires, who must act and from what date.
- Nothing is added that the order does not say.
- The brief refers to the order by its number and date.

#AHC-023 | legal office vocabulary and punctuation
- Legal writing uses words such as appellant, respondent, petitioner and opposite party with precise meanings.
- Case titles are written with the names of the parties separated by the word versus.
- Commas, semicolons and full stops are used carefully, because they can change the meaning of a sentence.
- Abbreviations are used only where the office style allows them.

#AHC-024 | mixed case numbers, dates and filing references
- This passage combines case numbers, filing references and dates in close succession.
- Each reference has a fixed pattern of letters, numbers and separators.
- The typist must keep every separator mark exactly as it appears.
- Dates are written in the full form used in court records.

#AHC-025 | a Group-C typing practice passage for district courts
- This practice passage reflects the clerical work of district court staff.
- It covers registers, notices and letters in formal English.
- The passage includes fictional names, case numbers and dates.
- Rules for the actual test must be taken from the notice for the recruitment cycle.
