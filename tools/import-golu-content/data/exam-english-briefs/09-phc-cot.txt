// PHC-COT — Patna High Court Computer Operator-cum-Typist (fictional Ganga Valley High Court Registry)

#PHC-COT-001 | a computer operator's daily work register
- The daily work register lists each document typed or entered by the operator with its file number.
- The time taken for long documents is recorded, which helps plan the workload.
- Documents returned for correction are noted with the reason.
- The register is signed by the section officer at the end of each day.

#PHC-COT-002 | receiving a court file in the registry
- A file received in the registry is checked for its number, the number of volumes and the page count.
- The receipt is entered in the movement register with the time and the sender's name.
- Missing pages or volumes are reported at once to the sending section.
- The file is then placed in the rack assigned to its category.

#PHC-COT-003 | preparing a court notice in formal English
- A court notice states the case number, the names of the parties and the date fixed for hearing.
- It tells the recipient what is required, such as appearing in person or filing a reply.
- The language is formal and free of unnecessary words.
- The notice is checked against the order before it is signed.

#PHC-COT-004 | verifying case numbers and filing dates
- Each case number in a list is compared with the registration register.
- Filing dates are checked against the stamp on the first page of the petition.
- Differences are marked and referred to the filing section.
- The verified list is initialled on every page.

#PHC-COT-005 | a typed list of pending matters
- The list of pending matters shows each case with its number, category and date of last hearing.
- Matters are sorted by age, so that the oldest appear first.
- The total number of pending matters is given at the end of the list.
- The list is updated every month from the case management system.

#PHC-COT-006 | cause list data and serial numbers
- Each matter in the cause list has a serial number that the court uses to call it.
- Serial numbers run without a break within each list.
- Supplementary lists start a new series, marked clearly at the top.
- The operator checks that no matter appears twice in the same list.

#PHC-COT-007 | the procedure for scanning documents and naming files
- Documents are scanned in the order in which they appear in the file.
- Each scanned file is named with the case number and the document type.
- The operator checks the scanned image for missing or blurred pages.
- Scanned files are saved in the folder assigned to the year of filing.

#PHC-COT-008 | a request to retrieve a digital case record
- A request for a digital case record names the case number and the document required.
- The operator searches the system and confirms that the requester is authorised.
- The record is shared through the approved channel and the request is logged.
- Records that cannot be found are reported with the search terms used.

#PHC-COT-009 | an office memorandum for court staff
- An office memorandum conveys an instruction or information to the staff of the court.
- It carries a number, a date and a subject line.
- The memorandum is brief and states clearly what staff must do and by when.
- It is circulated to all sections and kept in the memorandum file.

#PHC-COT-010 | a meeting agenda for registry officers
- The agenda lists the date, time and venue of the meeting and the items for discussion.
- Each item has a short note explaining the matter and the decision required.
- Papers relating to the items are attached in the same order.
- The agenda is sent to officers two days before the meeting.

#PHC-COT-011 | preparing a certified copy dispatch list
- The dispatch list records certified copies ready for delivery with the applicant's name and the copy number.
- Copies sent by post are entered with the postal receipt number.
- Copies collected in person are signed for in the delivery column.
- The list is closed at the end of each day and filed.

#PHC-COT-012 | court fee receipt and application tracking
- A court fee receipt is linked to the application for which the fee was paid.
- The tracking register shows the application number, the receipt number and the current stage.
- Applications delayed beyond the usual time are marked for follow-up.
- Applicants can ask for the stage by quoting the receipt number.

#PHC-COT-013 | a court website notice and recruitment information
- A website notice gives the subject, the date and the details that the public needs.
- Recruitment notices state the post, the number of vacancies and the last date for applying.
- Any change to an earlier notice is published as a separate corrigendum.
- The notice is checked carefully before upload, since it becomes a public record at once.

#PHC-COT-014 | a brief on the electronic filing of documents
- Electronic filing lets advocates and parties submit documents online.
- The system checks the file format and size before accepting a document.
- Each electronic filing receives an acknowledgement with a reference number.
- Scrutiny of an electronic filing follows the same rules as a paper filing.

#PHC-COT-015 | preparing an accurate court statistics table
- The statistics table shows cases instituted, disposed of and pending for each category.
- The pending figure equals the opening balance plus institutions minus disposals.
- Each column is totalled and checked against the case management system.
- A note explains any category in which the figures have changed sharply.

#PHC-COT-016 | entering case status and the next date
- After each hearing, the operator enters the stage of the case and the next date in the system.
- The entry is made from the signed order sheet, not from notes taken in court.
- The next date is checked against the court calendar to make sure it is a working day.
- The system shows the update on the public case status page.

#PHC-COT-017 | a formal letter requesting missing documents
- The letter identifies the case and the documents that are missing from the record.
- It requests the office concerned to send the documents by a stated date.
- The letter refers to the court's direction that requires the documents.
- A reminder is sent if no reply is received by the date given.

#PHC-COT-018 | a record room issue and return register
- The issue register records each file taken from the record room with the borrower's name and section.
- The date and time of return are entered when the file comes back.
- Files kept beyond the allowed period are listed for reminders.
- The register is checked monthly against the files on the shelves.

#PHC-COT-019 | judicial administration and timely case processing
- Timely processing of cases depends on prompt registration, listing and service of notices.
- Each section has time limits for its part of the work.
- Delays are identified through regular reports and addressed by the officers concerned.
- Efficient administration supports the court in deciding cases without avoidable delay.

#PHC-COT-020 | a note on confidential court information
- Confidential court information includes sealed documents and details protected by law.
- Such information is handled only by staff authorised to deal with it.
- It is never discussed outside the office or stored on personal devices.
- Breaches are reported at once to the officer in charge.

#PHC-COT-021 | a court calendar and holiday schedule
- The court calendar shows the working days, holidays and vacation periods for the year.
- Holidays are listed with their dates and the days of the week.
- Special sittings during vacations are announced by separate notices.
- The calendar is published at the start of the year and placed on the website.

#PHC-COT-022 | a computer equipment maintenance log
- The maintenance log records each fault reported with the equipment number, the date and the nature of the fault.
- The technician records the action taken and the date of repair.
- Equipment that fails repeatedly is listed for replacement.
- The log helps plan the purchase of spares and new equipment.

#PHC-COT-023 | proofreading a court office draft
- Proofreading compares the typed draft with the original word by word.
- Names, case numbers and dates receive special attention.
- Errors are marked with standard proof marks in the margin.
- The corrected draft is checked again before it is sent for signature.

#PHC-COT-024 | a court administration passage of about four hundred words
- This practice passage is of the length commonly used in a short court typing test.
- It describes routine registry work in clear and formal English.
- The typist should aim to complete it with very few errors within the time allowed.
- The length and time rules of an actual test must be taken from its notice.

#PHC-COT-025 | a ten-minute high court typing simulation
- This simulation gives a continuous passage of court-related prose for timed practice.
- It includes case numbers, dates and the names of fictional parties.
- The typist should keep a steady pace for the full duration.
- Rules for an actual test are configured separately in the app from the published notice.
