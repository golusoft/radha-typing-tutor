// BICC — Bihar Civil Courts clerical practice pool (fictional Kamalpur Civil Court Office)

#BICC-001 | a district court filing counter register
- The filing counter register records each document filed with a serial number, the case details and the filing party.
- The time of filing is noted, since limitation may depend on the exact date.
- Documents filed in a pending case are entered against that case number.
- The register is closed at the end of the counter hours and signed by the filing clerk.

#BICC-002 | receiving a petition and assigning a diary number
- Every petition received is first given a diary number in the diary register.
- The diary number links the petition to its later registration number.
- The diary register shows the date, the nature of the petition and the advocate's name.
- Petitions returned for defects keep their diary number when they are filed again.

#BICC-003 | a court date and hearing schedule list
- The hearing schedule lists the matters fixed before each court for every day of the week.
- Each line shows the case number, the stage and the time slot if one is given.
- Copies of the schedule are given to the reader of each court.
- Changes are written in the schedule in red and initialled by the clerk.

#BICC-004 | a summons dispatch and acknowledgement register
- Each summons sent out is entered with the case number, the addressee and the date of dispatch.
- Acknowledgements received from the post office are pasted or noted against the entry.
- Summonses returned without service are entered with the reason given.
- The register is checked before each hearing to see whether service is complete.

#BICC-005 | the status of a certified copy request
- A certified copy request moves through the stages of receipt, search, preparation and delivery.
- The status register shows the current stage of each request.
- Requests delayed because a record is with another section are marked with the section's name.
- The applicant can ask for the status by quoting the request number.

#BICC-006 | cause list preparation for a court day
- The cause list for a court day is prepared from the case diary of each court.
- Matters are listed in the order fixed by the presiding officer.
- The list is typed, checked and displayed outside the courtroom before the court sits.
- A copy is kept in the office as a record of the day's listing.

#BICC-007 | document pagination and an annexure index
- Every page of a case record is numbered in sequence before the record is bound.
- The annexure index shows each annexure, its description and the pages it covers.
- Loose papers are never left unnumbered inside a file.
- The clerk certifies the total number of pages on the index.

#BICC-008 | court fee and receipt details
- Court fee paid on each document is recorded with the amount and the receipt number.
- The fee is checked against the schedule that applies to the document.
- Deficit fee is noted and the party is informed of the amount to be paid.
- The fee register is balanced at the end of each month.

#BICC-009 | a clerk's daily work summary
- The daily work summary lists the filings received, the copies issued and the files moved.
- It notes any work left pending and the reason.
- The figures are taken from the registers of the day.
- The summary is submitted to the head clerk before the office closes.

#BICC-010 | moving a case file between sections
- When a case file moves from one section to another, the movement is entered in the file movement register.
- The receiving section signs for the file and notes the time of receipt.
- The file cover shows the current section in pencil, updated at every move.
- Lost files are traced through the movement register.

#BICC-011 | a court visitor enquiry and direction note
- Visitors at the enquiry counter are told which court their case is in and how to reach it.
- The enquiry clerk checks the case number in the register before giving directions.
- A simple map of the court building is kept at the counter.
- Visitors are not given information about cases in which they are not parties.

#BICC-012 | a record room file retrieval request
- A retrieval request names the case number, the year and the reason for which the file is needed.
- The record keeper finds the file using the record room index.
- The file is issued against a signature, and the date of return is fixed.
- Requests that cannot be met are answered with a note giving the reason.

#BICC-013 | a witness attendance register
- The witness attendance register records each witness who appears with the case number and the date.
- The time of arrival and the time of release are noted.
- Witnesses entitled to expenses are shown with the amount paid.
- The register is signed by the reader of the court at the end of the day.

#BICC-014 | a court holiday and working day notice
- The notice lists the holidays of the civil courts for the year.
- It mentions any working Saturdays and the arrangements for urgent matters.
- The notice is displayed at the entrance and in the bar room.
- Changes during the year are notified through a fresh notice.

#BICC-015 | preparing a list of pending applications
- The list shows every pending application with its number, the case it belongs to and the date of filing.
- Applications are grouped by court and sorted by date.
- The total number of pending applications is shown for each court.
- The list is updated weekly and sent to the presiding officers.

#BICC-016 | scanning and archiving old case files
- Old case files are scanned in their original order, page by page.
- Fragile pages are handled carefully and scanned without unfolding them by force.
- Scanned files are named with the case number and year and stored on the court server.
- The scanned record is checked against the paper file before the file is archived.

#BICC-017 | civil court procedure in general
- A civil case begins with the filing of a plaint and the payment of court fee.
- The court issues summons to the defendant, who files a written statement.
- The parties lead evidence, and the court hears arguments before deciding the case.
- The decree is drawn up in accordance with the judgment.

#BICC-018 | a formal letter to a government department
- A letter from the court office to a government department refers to the case and the court's order.
- It states clearly what the department is required to do or to send.
- The letter gives the date by which a reply is expected.
- A copy of the order is enclosed with the letter.

#BICC-019 | maintaining order in the court registry
- The registry follows a fixed arrangement for files, registers and counters.
- Each clerk is responsible for a defined set of registers.
- Registers are kept up to date every day, so that no backlog builds up.
- Visitors are served in turn at the counters.

#BICC-020 | updating case details in a digital register
- Case details are updated in the digital register after every hearing.
- The update includes the stage, the next date and a short note of the order.
- The clerk checks the case number before saving the update.
- The register is backed up at the end of each day.

#BICC-021 | checking case numbers and party names
- Each case number is checked against the institution register.
- Party names are compared with the plaint, including initials and the order of names.
- Spelling differences are noted and referred to the court if a correction is needed.
- The checked list is signed by the clerk.

#BICC-022 | preparing a daily dispatch statement
- The daily dispatch statement lists every letter and summons sent out on the day.
- It shows the dispatch number, the addressee and the mode of dispatch.
- The total number of items and the postage spent are given at the end.
- The statement is attached to the dispatch register.

#BICC-023 | the confidentiality and proper handling of court documents
- Court documents are handled only by staff who need them for their work.
- Documents are never left on open counters where visitors can read them.
- Sealed documents are kept in a separate locked cabinet.
- Copies are issued only through the copying section.

#BICC-024 | a short summary of court proceedings
- The summary records the date, the court, the parties present and the order passed.
- It is written in neutral and precise language.
- The summary is checked against the order sheet.
- It is placed on the file for future reference.

#BICC-025 | a civil court clerical English practice simulation
- This practice passage uses the language of civil court registers, notices and letters.
- It contains case numbers, dates and fictional names of parties.
- The passage is for general practice and is not linked to any official test rule.
- The typist should aim for accuracy first and speed second.
